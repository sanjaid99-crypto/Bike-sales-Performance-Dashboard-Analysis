# Bike Sales Dashboard - Power BI

This is my first end-to-end data analysis project. 
I picked a bike sales dataset and worked through the 
whole process - cleaning messy data, building pivot 
tables, and finally creating a Power BI dashboard.

## Why I did this

I'm learning data analysis on my own (through YouTube 
and free courses) and wanted a project that actually 
felt like something a company would ask for, not just 
another Kaggle notebook.

## What I worked with

The raw dataset had 90 rows and 19 columns, and it 
wasn't clean - there were blank cells, inconsistent 
gender values (M/F instead of Male/Female), and prices 
in USD when I wanted everything in INR. Cleaning this 
up in Excel taught me more than I expected, especially 
when I found duplicate country values that weren't 
showing up as duplicates because of hidden spaces - 
had to fix that in Power Query.

## Tools

- Excel - cleaning and pivot tables
- Power BI - dashboard and visuals

## What the dashboard shows

- Revenue and profit broken down by country
- Which day of the month sales peak (turns out day 19 
  had a big spike - might be linked to salary dates)
- Gender-wise revenue split
- Top performing products
- Interactive filters for country, gender, and age group

## What I found

- US brings in the most revenue (133K, about 37% of 
  total)
- Female customers account for 58% of revenue
- Mountain-200 Black is the top seller
- There's a clear spike around day 19 every month
